This is a personal website. It uses no cookies, advertising or comment services. Apart from the GoatCounter visitor counter described below, it loads no content from third parties.

## Hosting
The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as described in [What is GitHub Pages?](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages).

## Visitor statistics
I use [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com) to see how many people visit the site and which pages they read. GoatCounter stores nothing in your browser (no cookies or local storage) and does not store IP addresses. It keeps only aggregate counts of pages, referrers, browsers, operating systems, screen sizes and approximate locations, which are derived from the IP address. To tell unique visits apart, it holds a random identifier in memory for up to eight hours. The data is stored on servers in Finland and Germany and is not shared with third parties. See [GoatCounter's privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/privacy) for details.

## Settings saved in your browser
If you switch between the light and dark theme, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage. Interactive pages may also save your language choice and whether you have dismissed an introduction hint. These settings stay on your device.

## Email
If you email me, I use your message only to reply to you.
