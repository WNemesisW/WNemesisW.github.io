All IT shut down All servers were back by 21 Sep 2021, more than four months after the attack.

  1. 1

    Office breached

    A phishing email arrived on 16 March 2021, and an employee opened its Excel attachment on 18 March.

  2. 2

    Records encrypted

    The attacker spread to servers and hospitals in May and launched ransomware on 14 May 2021. All IT was switched off and the network cut from the internet.

    Conti; antivirus alerts on 13 May were not acted on

  3. 3

    Care on paper

    Laboratory, imaging and patient administration systems were lost, radiotherapy stopped at five centres, and care continued on paper.

What limited the damage: The attacker released a decryptor on 20 May; cloud systems were untouched, and no ransom was paid.

Care postponed Services were fully restored by December 2024.

  1. 1

    Laboratory encrypted

    The pathology provider's IT went down in a ransomware attack on 3 June 2024.

    Synnovis

  2. 2

    Diagnostics unavailable

    Blood cross-matching could not be done, so type O blood was used for transfusions.

  3. 3

    Care postponed

    The most affected trusts postponed 10,152 outpatient appointments and 1,710 elective procedures.

What limited the damage: Operations moved to other hospitals, blood tests were rerouted to other laboratories, and more type O blood was collected.

Emergency care closed The emergency department reopened on 23 Sep 2020.

  1. 1

    Perimeter breached

    The attacker got in through the remote access gateway. Updates had been installed in January 2020.

    Citrix NetScaler Gateway; DoppelPaymer

  2. 2

    Records encrypted

    About 30 servers were encrypted on the night to 10 Sep 2020, and the hospital information system stopped.

  3. 3

    Emergency closed

    The emergency department did not admit patients. One patient was sent to another city, her treatment was delayed by about an hour, and she died.

    According to the prosecutor the delay did not affect the outcome news source

What limited the damage: The attacker handed over a decryption key after police contacted them.

Dispensing was disrupted Payment processing was still at 86% of normal on 1 May.

  1. 1

    Intermediary breached

    Stolen credentials on a remote access portal without multi-factor authentication (12 Feb 2024).

    Citrix; ALPHV/BlackCat

  2. 2

    Medicines and follow-up stopped

    Ransomware on 21 Feb 2024 stopped claims, payments and pharmacy claims.

What limited the damage: The pharmacy network was restored first, 99% of pharmacies by 7 March, and volume was moved to competitors.

Manual routines Region Uppsala stayed in crisis management until 9 Feb 2024.

  1. 1

    IT service encrypted

    Ransomware hit an IT service provider's data centre in Sweden on the night to 20 Jan 2024.

    Tietoevry; Akira

  2. 2

    Records manual

    In Region Uppsala, patient records, finance and supply ordering ran on manual routines. In Sörmland, discharge coordination with municipalities was down.

What limited the damage: Contingency routines; Uppsala's patient records were fixed within days.

Patient records published Highly sensitive data on about 33,000 people leaked, and the provider went bankrupt.

  1. 1

    Records no password

    The psychotherapy provider's patient database was reachable from the internet without a password in 2017–2019, and it was copied on 26 Nov 2018.

  2. 2

    Records data taken

    Management was extorted in September 2020. In October data was published, and patients were extorted one by one.

  3. 3

    Care bankruptcy

    The provider went bankrupt in February 2021. The perpetrator was convicted, and the judgment is final.

Why this belongs in the chain: A leak of patient records does not stop care, but it can bring down the provider.
