Control lost At least 75 devices were compromised, at least 34 of them in the water sector.

  1. 1

    Distribution breached

    From 22 Nov 2023, the attackers logged in to internet-facing PLCs that had a default password or none at all.

    Unitronics Vision, TCP 20256; CyberAv3ngers (IRGC)

  2. 2

    Distribution control lost

    The attackers erased the program, renamed the devices and changed the port. A message on the panel hid the readings from operators.

Why this belongs in the chain: The PLC was directly on the internet without a password of its own. CISA urges taking such devices off the internet and changing passwords.

Valve open 4 h According to the dam owner, the extra flow had no other consequences.

  1. 1

    Raw water breached

    On 7 April 2025, the attacker got into the remote control panel of the dam's valve, probably through a weak password.

    Bremanger; video on Telegram, PST: pro-Russian group news source

  2. 2

    Raw water valve open

    The valve was opened fully for nearly four hours, releasing 497 l/s above the minimum flow.

What limited the damage: The dam is not used for power generation, and the extra flow stayed small.

    Water treatment breached

  2. 2

    Water treatment manual

    As a precaution, the plant switched to manual operation until the situation was resolved.

What limited the damage: Manual operation: the plant could be run without automation.

IT disrupted According to the company, the disruption did not affect the supply of safe water.

  1. 1

    Office breached

    South Staffordshire Water's IT network was disrupted in an extortion attack in August 2022.

    Clop; the group first named the wrong water company news source

  2. 2

    Control room claim

    The group claimed it had reached the control systems and taken 5 TB of data. The company did not confirm the claim.

    unconfirmed unverified claim

What limited the damage: Water production and distribution ran separately from the disrupted IT.

Billing paused According to the company, its water and wastewater facilities were not affected and the water was safe to drink.

  1. 1

    Billing breached

    The company detected unauthorised activity in its network on 3 October 2024.

  2. 2

    Customers billing paused

    The MyWater customer portal was shut down and billing paused; no late fees or shutoffs were made during the outage.

What limited the damage: Plant control was separate from the customer systems, so water supply continued.
