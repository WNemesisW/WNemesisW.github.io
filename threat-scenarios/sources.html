Sources and Shodan queries

An open port does not automatically mean a vulnerable device. The numbers are indicative.

What the number meansThe number of open services visible in Shodan in Finland on 11 Nov 2026. One result is a service at an address and port, not an identified device.
Some are honeypotsAbout 15–25% of internet-exposed industrial control systems are decoys (Mladenov et al. 2025). The numbers have not been adjusted.
A protocol does not reveal the sectorThe link to risks is the author's assessment. A Modbus device can be at a water utility, in a building or on a farm.
A single query, not a trendThe queries were run once with the scope country:FI and, for comparison, with the same queries for the other Nordic and Baltic countries. The numbers can be reproduced in Shodan.

Services that are public by design (NTP, BGP, DNS, SIP, IKE, AD FS, H.323, SMTP, WordPress and SAP) were also queried for comparison but are not shown on the map. The product queries Fronius, VSAT and GNSS-aika did not identify devices (0 results). Satellite terminals usually geolocate to the satellite operator's country, so a country scope does not find them.

Comparison: Nordic and Baltic countries

Per capita, Finland's exposure is above the median for 9 of 16 services and the highest of the eight countries for 5 (DICOM, MongoDB, PostgreSQL, SMB and RDP). The eight countries show 886,761 open services in total for these 16 queries, and 8.8% of them are in Finland.

Supervisory authorities

Finland's Cybersecurity Act requires operators in critical sectors to manage their cyber risks and to report significant incidents. Supervisors by sector (source: Kyberturvallisuuskeskus: Tärkeää tietoa EU:n kyberturvallisuusdirektiivistä (NIS2)).

SectorRisk on the mapSupervisor

energy (oil)Major fuel availability disruptionTukes
water supplyWater supply disruptionsEconomic Development Centre

digital infrastructureCommunications network disruptions and Disruption of data availabilityTraficom (NCSC-FI)
health care and pharmaceuticalsAntimicrobial resistance and Pandemic or major epidemicFinnish Supervisory Agency; pharmaceuticals Fimea
foodFood supply disruptionsFinnish Food Authority
banking and financial marketsFinancial system disruptionFinancial Supervisory Authority
spaceSpace service disruptionsTraficom
manufacturingValue and production chain disruptionsTukes, Traficom or Fimea depending on the industry

Linking a risk to a sector is the author's interpretation; the Act does not use the risk assessment's division. Significant incidents are reported without delay to the sector supervisor with the NCSC-FI NIS2 incident reporting application (early warning within 24 hours, notification within 72 hours). In finance, obligations may come from the DORA regulation instead. NCSC-FI: NIS2 FAQ (in Finnish) ↗
