iMethod and caveats
  • What the number means. The number of open services visible in Shodan in Finland in the latest query on 11 Nov 2026. One result is a service at an address and port, not an identified device.
  • An open port is not a vulnerable device. The numbers are indicative. About 15–25% of internet-exposed industrial control systems are honeypots (Mladenov et al. 2025); the numbers have not been adjusted.
  • A protocol does not reveal the sector. The link to a risk is the author's assessment of the risk assessment's text (strength and PDF page for each service).
  • A weekly query. The queries run weekly with the scope country:FI. Changes are calculated only between queries with the same query string.
  • Services that are public by design (NTP, BGP, DNS, SIP, IKE, AD FS, H.323, SMTP, WordPress and SAP) were queried for comparison but are not shown. The product queries Fronius, VSAT and GNSS-aika did not identify devices. Satellite terminals usually geolocate to the operator's country, so a country scope does not find them.
