Sources and Shodan queries

What the number meansThe number of open services visible in Shodan in Finland on 10 Nov 2026. One result is a service at an address and port, not an identified device.
Some are honeypotsAbout 15–25% of internet-exposed industrial control systems are decoys (Mladenov et al. 2025). The numbers have not been adjusted.
A protocol does not reveal the sectorThe link to risks is the author's assessment. A Modbus device can be at a water utility, in a building or on a farm.
A single query, not a trendThe query was run once with the scope country:FI. The numbers can be reproduced with the same query in Shodan.
