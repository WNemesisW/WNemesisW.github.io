iMethod and caveats
  • The same queries for eight countries. Queries with a country scope on 11 Nov 2026, relative to population in 2025 (World Bank). Rank 1 = most.
  • Country data is based on IP address location, so data centres and service providers affect the numbers. According to Shodan's organisation data, at least this share of Finland's results is in data centres and cloud services: MongoDB 83%, PostgreSQL 92%, SMB 64% and RDP 90%.
  • A small country (Iceland) does not support a precise comparison. Most of the eight countries' total are TR-069 devices.
