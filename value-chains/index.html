Model, not a real incident

What it meant for people

  • stopped Electricity: Power is cut over a wide area for a long time.
  • degraded IT and cloud: Digital services work only on backup power and while connections last.
  • severely degraded Logistics and fuel: Fuel distribution and deliveries stop.
  • severely degraded Telecoms: Calls, internet and the emergency number work only while backup power lasts.
  • severely degraded Water: Tap water and sewerage deteriorate when pumping stops.
  • degraded Healthcare: Care continues on backup power, but only the essentials.
  • severely degraded Banking and payments: Cards and online payments fail, and ATMs give no cash.
  • severely degraded Food: Food is hard to buy, and chilled products spoil.
  1. 1

    Electricity cut

    A wide and long power cut. Backup power exists only at some sites and for a limited time.

  2. 2

    Telecoms on backup power

    Base stations go dark when batteries run out, pumping stops, data centres switch to backup power and fuel distribution halts.

  3. 3

    Healthcare degraded

    The hospital runs on backup power, but water, patient records and medicine deliveries deteriorate.

  4. 4

    Banking and payments degraded

    Card payments, ATMs and online banking do not work without power and connections.

  5. 5

    Food degraded

    The cold chain breaks, shops cannot sell without tills and payments, and deliveries stop.

Why this belongs in the chain: The model is based on the dependency sources, in particular the German Bundestag blackout study (Petermann et al. 2011). The levels illustrate the course, they do not measure a real incident.

Shipments stopped

What it meant for people

  • route IT and cloud: The malware spread through software and destroyed computers.
  • no data Banking and payments: Ukraine's financial sector was a primary target; the source does not report any effect on payments.
  • no data Electricity: Ukraine's energy sector was a primary target; the source does not report any effect on electricity supply.
  • degraded Food: The food company's deliveries to shops stood still for four days and were delayed; production continued at reduced capacity.
  1. 1

    IT and cloud breached

    Destructive malware was launched in Ukraine on 27 June 2017.

    UK: the Russian military

  2. 2

    Electricity target

    The primary targets were Ukraine's financial, energy and government sectors.

  3. 3

    Food shipments stopped

    The malware spread beyond Ukraine. At Mondelez, sales, distribution and finance networks stopped, and shipments and invoicing stood still for the last four days of the quarter.

What limited the damage: Continuity plans; Mondelez caught up on the delayed shipments in the following quarter.

Main source: UK FCO 15.2.2018: Foreign Office Minister condemns Russia for NotPetya attacks ↗·Sources →

Remote control lost

What it meant for people

  • degraded Telecoms: Tens of thousands of satellite internet users lost their connection; Viasat shipped nearly 30,000 replacement modems.
  • working Electricity: Electricity was produced normally; only remote monitoring and control of about 5,800 wind turbines was lost.
  1. 1

    Telecoms breached

    Into the management network through the satellite operator's misconfigured VPN appliance; from there the memory of tens of thousands of modems was overwritten on 24 Feb 2022.

    Viasat KA-SAT; AcidRain

  2. 2

    Electricity link cut

    Remote monitoring and control of about 5,800 wind turbines was lost.

What limited the damage: The turbines kept running automatically. Some parks moved to the mobile network.

Main source: Viasat: KA-SAT Network cyber attack overview ↗·Sources →

Stores closed

What it meant for people

  • route IT and cloud: Through remote management and the till service, the malware spread to the stores' tills.
  • severely degraded Food: About 700 of Coop's 800 shops were closed, and no food could be bought there; all were open again within six days.
  1. 1

    IT and cloud breached

    The attacker used zero-day vulnerabilities in remote management software on 2 July 2021.

    Kaseya VSA; REvil

  2. 2

    IT and cloud spread

    A service company that maintains stores' tills through remote management spread the malware to its customers.

    Visma EssCom

  3. 3

    Food closed

    Tills and self-checkouts were encrypted, and the stores could not take payments.

What limited the damage: Over 700 store servers were reinstalled through automation; customer data was not affected.

Main source: Visma: Kaseya-attacken (3.7.2021) ↗·Sources →

Done by hand

What it meant for people

  • stopped IT and cloud: The IT provider's customers' systems were down; over 90% of servers were restored in four days.
  • degraded Healthcare: Care continued, but in the Uppsala region patient records, finances and supply orders were handled by hand for about three weeks.
  • working Banking and payments: Payments worked normally; only the central bank's own payroll was down, and salaries were paid through a fallback procedure.
  1. 1

    IT and cloud encrypted

    Ransomware hit an IT service provider's data centre in Sweden on the night of 20 January 2024.

    Tietoevry; Akira

  2. 2

    Healthcare by hand

    In the Uppsala region, patient records, the finance system and supply ordering were handled by hand.

  3. 3

    Banking and payments disrupted

    The Riksbank's payroll system was unavailable; critical financial system services were not affected.

What limited the damage: Fallback procedures; over 90% of servers were restored from backups in four days.

Main source: Region Uppsala: IT-störningar (21.1.2024) ↗·Sources →

Access to medicines hampered

What it meant for people

  • stopped Banking and payments: Healthcare billing, payments and pharmacy claims stopped; in early May, payment traffic was at 86% of normal.
  • severely degraded Healthcare: 74% of hospitals in a survey reported a direct impact on patient care, and getting medicines from pharmacies became harder.
  1. 1

    Banking and payments breached

    Stolen credentials to the remote access portal of a healthcare payments clearinghouse that had no multi-factor authentication (12 Feb 2024).

    Citrix; ALPHV/BlackCat

  2. 2

    Healthcare stopped

    Ransomware on 21 Feb 2024 stopped billing, payments and pharmacy claims.

What limited the damage: The pharmacy network was restored first, 99% of pharmacies by 7 March, and traffic was routed to competitors.

Main source: Andrew Witty, written testimony to the US Senate (2024) ↗·Sources →

Not an attack

What it meant for people

  • stopped IT and cloud: About 8.5 million Windows devices crashed and had to be fixed by hand.
  • degraded Banking and payments: Some banking services went down: accounts, payments, cash withdrawals and online banking; most recovered the same day.
  • degraded Healthcare: In England most GP practices worked on paper and had to rearrange appointments; most hospital care continued as normal.
  1. 1

    IT and cloud faulty update

    A faulty security software update crashed about 8.5 million Windows devices in July 2024.

  2. 2

    Banking and payments outages

    Account access, payments, cash withdrawals and online banking logins were disrupted.

  3. 3

    Healthcare on paper

    In England, the appointment and patient record system was disrupted at most GP practices, which used paper records and handwritten prescriptions.

    EMIS

What limited the damage: Most financial institutions recovered the same day; most hospital care continued as normal.

Main source: Microsoft 20.7.2024: Helping our customers through the CrowdStrike outage ↗·Sources →
