What the incidents show

  • The widest impact came through trusted update and remote management channels: NotPetya, SolarWinds, Kaseya, 3CX and CrowdStrike.
  • Healthcare most often failed through a non-clinical supplier: the laboratory, payments or a data centre.
  • Food retail stopped through the till supplier, not through production.
  • Supply chain incidents affecting power have been losses of monitoring, not blackouts.
  • No supply chain incident spreading across sectors was found for water. The gap may be real or a lack of reporting.
