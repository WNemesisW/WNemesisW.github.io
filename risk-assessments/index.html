The comparison covers the risks in the national risk assessments of Finland, Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Iceland, Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania. The countries name and scope their risks differently, so each risk is mapped to one or more of 39 shared themes.

The assessments are not like for like. They were made in different years (2011–2026), for different purposes and with different methods, and some are only partly public. The comparison shows what each country has written into its public assessment, not the countries' actual level of risk or preparedness. Differences by country →

How to read the comparison

  1. By themeEach row is a theme and each column a country. A cell lists the country's risks in that theme; a dash means the assessment has no separate entry for it.
  2. By countryEach country's risks in the assessment's own order, with their themes below.
  3. Colours in nearly all assessments (at least 6 of 8), in some, in one country's assessment only.
  4. DetailsPoint at a risk: all its occurrences are highlighted. Click a risk to open a description and a link to the right page of the assessment above the comparison.
